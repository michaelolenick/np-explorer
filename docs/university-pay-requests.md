# University pay data requests

Ready-to-send emails for public-pay datasets that are only available on request. Nothing here has been sent by a script;
the main session sends them. Record the send date and any reply under each request.

## 1. Washington State Employee Salary Database (LEAP) - send now

- **To:** leap@leg.wa.gov
- **Why this address:** the "Download Data" link on https://fiscal.wa.gov/Staffing/Salaries.aspx is a `mailto:` to
  leap@leg.wa.gov with the subject "WA State Employee Salary Data"; its prefilled body says to send the email to receive
  a link to a download file and to include name, organization, telephone and email for future updates.
- **Dataset:** State Employee Salary Database (Legislative Evaluation and Accountability Program Committee, data from the
  Office of Financial Management). Per the site's FAQ (SalaryDataFAQ.pdf, Oct 2021) it has names, job titles and total
  calendar-year earnings for most state employees, including all public four-year institutions and community and technical
  colleges, for the previous five calendar years, rounded to the nearest $100; updated annually.
- **What we need:** the full download file, every calendar year in it (currently expected 2021-2025), all agencies and
  institutions, CSV or Excel. Our existing WA data (data.wa.gov y3ds-rkew) stops at 2013, so we also ask about earlier years.
- **Status:** not sent.

**Subject:** WA State Employee Salary Data

**Body:**

> Hello,
>
> I am writing to request the download link for the Washington State Employee Salary Database published on
> fiscal.wa.gov (State Staffing > State Employee Salaries > Download Data).
>
> Specifically, I would like the complete file for all agencies and higher education institutions (including the
> public four-year institutions and the community and technical colleges), for every calendar year the database
> currently contains, in CSV or Excel format, with the fields shown on the site (agency or institution, employee name,
> job title, and total calendar-year earnings for each year).
>
> If earlier years that have rotated out of the five-year window (for example 2014 through 2020) are kept in an archive,
> I would be grateful for those files as well, in the same format.
>
> The data will be used for non-commercial research on compensation at public colleges and universities at
> strategystudio.org. Please also add me to the list for future updates of this database.
>
> Thank you,
>
> Michael Olenick
> Strategy Studio
> michael@strategystudio.org

## 2. DC Department of Human Resources - UDC FY2016 and FY2019 listings (next round; do not send yet)

- **To:** sienna.haslup@dc.gov (DCHR FOIA Officer, Office of the General Counsel, 1015 Half Street SE, 8th Floor,
  Washington, DC 20003; (202) 442-9697)
- **Cc:** dchr@dc.gov (DCHR general address)
- **Why this address:** DCHR's "Open Government and FOIA" page (https://dchr.dc.gov/page/open-government-and-foia-dchr)
  names Sienna Haslup as FOIA Officer and says requests may be sent by email (or through the DC Public FOIA Portal,
