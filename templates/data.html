Data

Sources

  • IRS Form 990 e-file XML releases: the returns themselves, including officers, directors, trustees, key employees and their compensation.
  • IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File: organization names, EINs, locations and status.
  • ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API, where used, to cross-check organizations and link to filings.

Coverage

  • E-filed returns only. Organizations that filed on paper are absent.
  • Small organizations that file the 990-N postcard report no people, so they have no people data here.
  • Coverage starts with the earliest year the IRS publishes as e-file XML.

Name matching

Many people share a name. People with the same name are shown as separate records unless the evidence links them, such as the same organization, an overlapping title, or a consistent address across filings.

Every link between records shows its evidence, so you can judge it yourself. When the evidence is thin, we keep the records apart rather than guess.
