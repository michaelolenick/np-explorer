About

Who

Built by Michael Olenick.

I had the fortune, or misfortune, of working on the financial crisis with Congress, the Treasury, the Federal Reserve and a small army of attorneys. Today I work with the media, government, academia and plenty of advocates. A former lead attorney once called me “a dangerous guy.” I take it as a compliment: I find problems and solve them.

I have a law degree but don't practice. I write and run other systems, but this one solves a real pain point. It was, and still is, a labor of love that I built for myself. I thought the world might be interested too.

Why

People say they run nonprofits, sit on boards, draw no salary. Checking takes an hour of digging through IRS filings. Most people don't bother.

Because of abuse, people don't donate. As the saying goes, sunlight is said to be the best of disinfectants. We strongly encourage supporting charities and hope our system makes it more likely people will do so to those most deserving.

This site does the digging, with much faster results than Claude, ChatGPT, and the others. Type a name and see every Form 990 that person appears on, with the filing linked so you can read it yourself.

What it is not

  • Not affiliated with the IRS or ProPublica.
  • Not legal, financial or tax advice.
  • Not more accurate than the filings. The data is only as good as what organizations reported to the IRS.
