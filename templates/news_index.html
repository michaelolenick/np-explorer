In the news

FURP matches the organizations and people that news articles name to its own records, so every organization and person page shows the latest articles that mention it, and this page ranks who is in the news. Articles come from the GDELT Project, which reads news in over 100 languages every 15 minutes; FURP keeps each article's link, title, date and tone, never its text.
