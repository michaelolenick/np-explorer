IPO registrations (S-1, F-1)

Companies going public, or registering any public offering of their own stock, file Form S-1 (or F-1 when foreign) with the SEC, then amendments as the SEC comments, a final prospectus (424B4 or 424B1) with the price once the deal is done, and the SEC posts a notice when the registration is declared effective. Here is every one of those filings in EDGAR’s index since 1993, grouped by company, with each company tied to its insiders, Form D offerings, nonprofits, state registrations, lobbying and other records on this site. Each filing links to EDGAR.

What this covers, and what it doesn't

  • Forms S-1, S-1/A, F-1 and F-1/A from EDGAR’s quarterly full index, 1993 to now, every filer. Shelf registrations (S-3), small-company offerings (Regulation A, Form 1-A), SPAC mergers on S-4 and registrations before EDGAR (1993, with full coverage from 1996) are not here.
  • The final prospectus (424B4, 424B1), notice of effectiveness (EFFECT), Rule 462(b) add-ons (S-1MEF, F-1MEF) and withdrawal requests (RW) are included when filed between the company’s first S-1 or F-1 and two years after its last; a 424B4 for an unrelated shelf offering in that window is counted too.
  • The offering terms (price range, final price, shares, proceeds, exchange and ticker, underwriters) are read from the cover pages of the final prospectus and the last registration statement before pricing, with regular expressions; a cover page laid out unusually can leave a term blank or wrong, and each company page links the documents read. The registrant’s financial statements are not parsed. Company details (SIC, state of incorporation, address, EIN, tickers, former names) are what EDGAR holds today, not at the time of the filing.
  • Ties to people and organizations on this site by name and state are marked Fuzzy; ties by CIK or EIN are marked EXACT.
