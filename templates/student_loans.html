Student loan securitizations

Trusts that hold pools of student loans and sell bonds backed by them file a servicing report with the SEC every month (Form 10-D, or Form 8-K before 2006). These pages show what those reports say: how much the pool still owes, how many loans are behind, and how many defaulted or were charged off.

Only trusts registered with the SEC appear. Many private and refinance deals (SoFi, College Ave, Navient and SLM refinance trusts after 2014, most of Earnest, Laurel Road and CommonBond) are sold privately under Rule 144A and file nothing public, and state and nonprofit student loan agencies usually issue municipal bonds reported to the MSRB, not the SEC. Student loans are not covered by the SEC's loan-level (Form ABS-EE) rules, so no loan-level data exists here: every figure is a pool total.
