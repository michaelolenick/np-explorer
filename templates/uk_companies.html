What this covers, and what it doesn't

  • Live companies only: the free product leaves out dissolved companies. Officers (directors and secretaries) are not published as a free bulk file, so none are shown; Companies House's API serves them with a free key.
  • PSC names, nationality, country of residence, birth year and the nature of control are on the public register and shown here; an individual's address and day of birth are not kept. The register has no identifier that follows one person across companies, so “the same person” below means the same name and birth month and year.
  • Cross-references to US businesses, SEC filers and people say how they were made. A name alone never links a UK company to a US one, and a PSC's name alone never links to a person page. How the matching works.
