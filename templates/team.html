About

Team

Michael Olenick, Founder

A non-practicing attorney who helped crack the robosigning scandal with documents, Michael went on to work at INSEAD for 12 years before returning to the US. Michael has a strong belief that information is power, but that in its current form it is disorganized. FURP is the attempt to organize it.

Mike Leidig, NewsX

A British journalist and author based in Austria. Mike Leidig founded the Vienna-based news agency Central European News in 1995, launched the Fourth Estate Alliance in 2018 to support freelance journalists, and later founded NewsX, a community interest company that supports independent journalism.

Mike is the co-author of Girl in the Cellar (with Allan Hall) and the author of Pyramid Games, about European connections to the Madoff scandal, and has campaigned for fairer pay for editorial work.

If you have a strong belief in open data, contact us. We are always looking for help: info@furp.org.
