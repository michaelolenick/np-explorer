We saw a problem. People say they run nonprofits, sit on boards, draw no salary. Checking means an hour of digging through IRS filings. Most people don't bother.

Nonprofit people say the drag is finding the money. We thought a bigger drag was finding the people asking for money, who they are and how much they're paid. So we fixed that.

We're fixing the problem. Type a name. See the person: every organization they're tied to, every title, every year, every dollar. Links to the organizations and the filings are there if you want them.
