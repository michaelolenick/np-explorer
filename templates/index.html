We saw a problem. People say they run nonprofits, sit on boards, draw no salary. Checking takes an hour of digging through IRS filings. Most people don't bother.

We're fixing the problem. Type a name. See every Form 990 that person appears on: every organization, every title, every year, every dollar, with the filing linked so you can read it yourself.

This is a nonprofit in the original sense: it doesn't make money. It just lets you check the ones that say they don't.

Free. Open source. No ads, no login, no sales pitch.

Google could have built this any time in the last twenty years. Just saying.
