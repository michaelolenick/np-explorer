UK charities

The register of charities for England and Wales, kept by the Charity Commission, treated like the American nonprofit data: one page per charity with its income and spending over the years, its trustees (each a person page that lists every charity they serve), what it does and where, and a cross-reference to the US nonprofits, grants and officers that match it.

What this covers, and what it doesn't

  • England and Wales only. Scotland (OSCR) and Northern Ireland (CCNI) publish their own registers, not loaded here.
  • {{ ukc.SCOPE_NOTE }}
  • Trustees carry the Commission's own trustee number, so one person who serves several charities is one page. Matches to US people need the same name on a US nonprofit that is itself matched to the charity (by website, an “American Friends of” name, or the same name), never the name alone.
  • US matches say how they were made and how sure they are; same-name-only matches are shown as possible and are often different organizations. How the matching works.
